Siargao General Luna

The most social island in the country, with great surf culture and coworking. But January is its rainy month.

👥5Social
✨5Experiences
💻5Work
☀️1Jan weather
🚐4Ease
  • 🏝️Tri-island boat day: Naked, Daku & GuyamNaked Island: G 4.5★ (713)
  • 🛶Sugba Lagoon day tripG 4.5★ (962)
  • 🌊Magpupungko rock pools at low tideG 4.5★ (1,489)

🙋 Meet people, never forced

  • Mad Monkey Siargao Free-flow “Meet Up” at 7pm every night, plus quiz and challenge nights. Hostelworld lists 38 events. Dorms only, so do 2 nights here for the buzz, then move.
  • Surf camps Kermit Surf Resort runs surf-camp packages. Surf & Yoga Siargao is an all-inclusive camp (G 4.8★, 73).
  • Coworking crowd Coco Space and Lungga are coworking and community spaces. You meet people just by working there.
  • Easy exit Stay somewhere calmer (Bajala or Kermit) and dip into the social stuff only when you feel like it.

🛂 Staying longer than 30 days

🇦🇺
30 days visa-free for Australians. Arrive 26 Dec, so day 30 is about 24 Jan. You’ll need an extension to stay to 5 Feb. BI
🏢
Extend to 59 days: a “Visa Waiver” at any Bureau of Immigration office (e.g. Cebu District Office, 4F Gaisano Mall, or the Dumaguete Field Office). The fee is ₱3,030 (≈ A$69). Go a few days early and take your passport, onward ticket and a photo. BI visa waiver page
➕
Second month? Past 59 days you extend again: roughly ₱4,400 for 1 month or ₱4,900 for 2 (the amount varies by office), plus an ACR I-Card (US$50) and ₱500 express. That’s ≈ A$180–195. BI extensions go up to 36 months in total.
💻
Digital nomad visa: it was signed into law (EO 86, 24 Apr 2025) but is not open yet. There are no implementing rules and no way to apply as of Sep 2026. EO 86 · status · Genki

Four bases that stay on the drier side, catch Sinulog and the La Union surf comp, and mix social with quiet.

≈ 6–12 Jan

2

Moalboal → Cebu City

Sardines and canyoneering with the Chief Mau crowd, then 2 nights in Cebu for Sinulog (16–17 Jan). Do your visa extension at BI Cebu while you’re there.

3
≈ 18–28 Jan
La Union

4

Baguio or Sagada

Cool mountain air to finish: a Mt Pulag sunrise, cafés and caves. Back to Manila for flights, with Chinese New Year in Binondo on 6 Feb if you’re still around.

🧮 How the scores work

Each score is 1–5 from a fixed rule, and each card’s “Why these scores” shows the numbers. Fit = Social×3 + Experiences×2 + Work + Weather + Ease (max 40). Ratings: “G 4.7★ (88)” = Google, 88 reviews. “HW 9.6 (159)” = Hostelworld. All checked 28 Sep 2026.

👥 Social (easy but optional) ×3 in Fit

Base from the best social hostel. 4: Hostelworld Atmosphere ≥9.5, ≥500 reviews and ≥30 listed events. 3: Atmosphere ≥9.0 with ≥300 reviews or ≥30 events, or Google ≥4.0★ with ≥1,000 reviews and regular events. 2: a hostel ≥4.3★ Google or ≥9.0 HW with ≥80 reviews but no event programme. 1: none. +1 if there are 2 or more other opt-in options: a surf school (≥4.4★, ≥50 reviews), community coworking (≥4.5★), a group-tour operator (≥4.5★, ≥100 reviews), or a major festival inside 5 Jan–5 Feb.

Data: Hostelworld property pages (Atmosphere sub-score, review count, “N events” badge) and Google Maps, 28 Sep 2026. Last ~10 Hostelworld reviews used for the age-group and “mentions vibe or friendly” shares.

✨ Experiences ×2 in Fit

2 + the number of the top-3 experiences rated ≥4.5★ on Google with ≥150 reviews. So 5 = all three are proven crowd-pleasers. A place’s top 3 were chosen before looking up ratings.

Data: Google Maps rating and review count for each named attraction.

💻 Work-friendly ×1 in Fit

5: 3 or more coworkings ≥4.5★ with backup or solar power. 4: 2 such coworkings, or 1 plus city fibre. 3: 1 coworking ≥4.5★ on an island. 2: no coworking ≥4.5★, so you rely on your stay’s wifi or Starlink. 1: power cuts or weak signal widely reported.

Data: Google Maps ratings. Coworking websites and 2026 nomad guides for power and Starlink notes. Speed tests are mostly unpublished, which is flagged where so.

☀️ January weather ×1 in Fit

Average January days with 5mm+ rain. 5: ≤3 · 4: ≤6 · 3: ≤10 · 2: ≤14.5 · 1: more.

Data: ERA5 reanalysis via Open-Meteo, Januaries 2016–2025, at each place’s coordinates. Model data tends to run wetter than ground stations, so use it to compare places, not as a forecast.

🚐 Easy to get to ×1 in Fit

Hours on the move from Manila or Cebu, whichever is better (flight, bus, boat and transfer, not counting airport waiting). 5: ≤2h · 4: 2–4h · 3: 4–6h · 2: 6–9h · 1: over 9h.

Data: Operator pages and 2026 transport guides (linked on each card).

Where a number isn’t known (wifi speeds, some prices, flight fares), the card says so instead of guessing. AUD prices were converted at 1 AUD = US$0.70 = ₱43.8 (28 Sep 2026).
